# My agent: FitCoach AI
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps fitness enthusiasts track workouts, calculate training zones, and plan personalized routines with a catalog of exercise routines and target workouts.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User fitness goals, past workout logs, target body focus, and injury history across sessions.
- Tools: Exercise lookup by muscle group, workout logging, and training zone calculation.
- Catalog/UI: Exercise catalog and daily workout plan cards with sets, reps, and target heart rate tables.
- Image gen: Custom motivational visual progress cards and form diagram visuals.
- Sandbox: Calculation of target heart-rate zones (Karvonen formula), 1RM (one-rep max) estimates, and macro split calculations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for target heart rate and 1RM calculations, Cloud Trace for observability.
